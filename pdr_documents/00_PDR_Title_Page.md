## Mission Overview

The Lunar PSR Autonomy Scout (LPAS) is a robotic precursor rover mission designed to characterize the volatile inventory, regolith properties, and operational environment within and around Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSRs) at the lunar south pole. LPAS directly supports Artemis program human landing site selection and in-situ resource utilization (ISRU) planning by providing ground-truth measurements of water ice concentration, distribution, and accessibility.

**Launch Target:** NET Q4 2028
**Landing Site:** Nobile Crater Region, 85.2°S, 32.0°E (primary); Haworth Crater (secondary)
**Mission Lifetime:** 120 sols (4 months) primary mission
**Rover Wet Mass:** 202 kg
**Power System:** Solar photovoltaic + LFP battery, 200W peak
**Science Payload Mass:** 22 kg

## Mission Success Criteria

**Minimum Mission Success:**
- Complete checkout and initial surface operations (Sol 0–7)
- Conduct at least one PSR ingress to a depth of ≥ 50 m
- Return ≥ 500 MB science data to Earth
- Characterize volatile content at ≥ 1 PSR site

**Full Mission Success:**
- Complete all six mission phases (Sol 0–120)
- Characterize volatile content at ≥ 3 PSR sites
- Return ≥ 5 GB science data to Earth
- Cache surface sample for potential Artemis crew retrieval
